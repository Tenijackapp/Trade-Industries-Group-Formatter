Schedule Formatter

Formats construction programmes & schedules for import into the JACK App job-schedules template. Reads MS-Project PDFs, Excel & Gantt exports. (build 1)

Open a schedule PDF (MS-Project programme), an Excel/CSV, or paste rows from Excel. The output always matches the JACK job-schedules columns.
